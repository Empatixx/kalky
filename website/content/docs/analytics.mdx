The Analytics tab answers the question the home screen cannot: not what you ate
today, but what you have been eating.

## Intake over a period
<Screenshot
  src="/img/en/analytics.png"
  alt="Analytics screen with weight chart and average intake"
  caption="Weight and average intake over the selected range."
/>


Pick a start and end date. For that range the app shows your **average daily
intake** in calories, along with average protein, average carbohydrates and
average fat.

Averages are the point here. One heavy day tells you nothing; a fortnight of
them tells you something. If the range has no logged calories in it, the screen
says so and sends you to the home screen to add some.

## Weight

Weight is charted over the same period, with your **current weight** and your
**average weight** called out. You add weight readings in Settings — until you
do, the chart is empty and says as much.

Reading intake and weight together is what makes the tab useful. A calorie
target is a prediction; your weight over several weeks is the measurement that
tells you whether the prediction was right.
