---
title: Logging food
description: Four ways to get a meal into the diary — photo, barcode, search, and manual entry.
---

Tap the button at the bottom of the home screen to add food. There are four
routes in, and they suit different situations.

## Photograph a meal

The camera opens in photo mode. Frame the plate and take the picture. The image
goes to the backend, which asks a vision model what the food is and what it is
likely to contain, and the result lands in your diary as an entry with
calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat.

The estimate is a guess from a picture. A plate photographed from an angle that
hides half the rice will report less rice. Treat the number as a reasonable
starting point and correct it — see [Daily overview](/en/docs/daily-overview) for
editing an entry.

<Callout type="warn">
  Photo analysis, product search and barcode lookup all talk to the Kalky
  backend. The public instance has been shut down, so a build of this
  open-source project needs its own backend — the repository README explains how
  to run one.
</Callout>

## Scan a barcode

Switch the camera to code mode and point it at the barcode on the packaging.
The product is looked up in the Kalky product database, and if it is not there,
in Open Food Facts. Once found, enter how many grams you ate and it is added.

If nothing matches the code, the app tells you the product was not found. Add
it by hand instead.

## Search for a food

Type a name and search the product database. Results are capped at twenty, so a
narrower query beats a broad one. The **My foods** tab holds foods you saved
yourself, and **Recently used** lists what you have logged lately, which is
usually the fastest way to re-add a regular meal.

## Add it manually

Enter the name and the nutrition per 100 g, then the portion size you ate. Use
this when the food is homemade, when the barcode lookup fails, or when you know
the numbers and want them exact.

Anything already in your diary can be kept for later with **Save as food**,
which puts it under My foods.
