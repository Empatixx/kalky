The home screen is the diary for one day. The header carries the date, and you
can move between days from there; adding food snaps the view back to today.

## What is on it

**Added today** lists the entries for the selected day. Each one shows the food,
its photo if there is one, and its calories.

**Daily totals** compare what you have eaten against your calorie target, with
protein, carbohydrates and fat broken out.

**Your streak** counts consecutive days on which you logged something — shown
as "1 day streak" or "%d day streak".

If a day is empty, the screen says so and points you at the button that adds
your first food.

Open an entry to see its detail: the photo, the nutrition, and a health quality
rating. From there you can **Fix** the numbers if the estimate was off,
**Share** the entry, or **Delete** it.

While an entry is being analysed the app shows that macros are still being
computed, and a food it could not identify appears as an unknown food. Both
resolve once analysis finishes.

## Working with several entries

Select multiple entries to delete them together — the header switches to a
selection mode showing how many are selected.

## Changing your targets

**Edit macronutrients** sets your daily calorie target and the split across
protein, carbohydrates and fat. Changes apply from that point on; days already
logged keep the totals they had.
