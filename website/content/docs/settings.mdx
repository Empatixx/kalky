Light, dark, or follow the system.

## Language

Czech or English. This changes the app's own text; it does not translate the
names of foods you have already logged.

## Units

Metric or imperial, affecting how weight and height are entered and displayed.

## Profile
<Screenshot
  src="/img/en/profile.png"
  alt="Profile screen with personal details and BMI"
  caption="Profile holds the inputs behind your daily targets."
/>


Weight, height, age, gender and activity level — the inputs behind your daily
targets, so changing them changes what the app expects you to eat. Profile also
shows your BMI with a band: underweight, normal, overweight or obese.

## Meal reminders

Notifications that nudge you when you have not logged anything, or when you are
behind on the day. They are off until you enable them and grant the permission.

## Account

Kalky signs you in with Google or Apple. Your account is what ties your data to
you; **Sign out** is here too.

## Legal

The terms of use and the privacy policy are both readable in the app.
