The first time you open Kalky it walks you through a short setup. Everything
you pick here can be changed later in Settings and Profile.

## Onboarding
<Screenshot
  src="/img/shared/onboarding-language.png"

/>


The steps are, in order:

1. **Choose language** — Czech or English.
2. **Choose units** — metric or imperial.
3. **Choose appearance** — light, dark, or follow the system.
4. **Your details** — gender, weight, height and age.
5. **How active are you?** — from sedentary through light and active to very
   active.
6. **What's your goal?** — lose weight, maintain weight, or gain weight.
7. **Your daily targets** — the result, which you can adjust.

There is also an optional promo code field near the end. Skip it if you do not
have one.

## How the targets are calculated

Kalky estimates your basal metabolic rate from your gender, weight, height and
age, scales it by your activity level to get your total daily energy
expenditure, then shifts it according to your goal. The macronutrient split is
derived from the resulting calorie target.

The app labels these numbers as indicative, and that is the right way to read
them. They are a starting point from a formula, not a prescription — if the
trend over a few weeks is not going where you want, adjust them.

## Changing your targets later

Open **Edit macronutrients** to set the daily calorie target and the split
between protein, carbohydrates and fat directly. Personal details live under
**Profile**, which also shows your BMI with a band from underweight through
normal and overweight to obese.
